Free Use and Distribution Agreement for BibliaTodo Translations
1.7
Effective from 2026-11-12

1. Purpose and scope
BibliaTodo LLC, through its publishing imprint BibliaTodo Bible Society, grants free permission to use its published original translations to help make the Word of God accessible worldwide. This agreement covers the rights BibliaTodo LLC can authorize in its own contributions. It claims no exclusive rights in ancient Scripture, the public domain, or third-party resources. It covers NTBT, BTNT and NTB-BR and future original translations expressly published under this agreement, across all permitted platforms and uses, including websites, Android, iOS and offline reading. The official pages identify the materials actually available.

2. Personal reading and downloads
Reading, downloading, and printing for personal use are free and require no registration or approval. A BibliaTodo Plus subscription is unnecessary. Statutory exceptions and limitations, including legally permitted quotations, remain in effect.

3. Registration by use and an optional certificate
The uses directly permitted in section 4 are available under this public agreement without registration, email verification, or an individual request. To distribute the complete print editions described there, the responsible party must register free of charge, verify their email address, and obtain written approval from BibliaTodo before printing.

Anyone who wants a personal certificate of the public permissions may request one through free registration and email verification. The certificate documents the accepted agreement and does not replace approval of a print edition. Both the certificate and editorial updates are optional. A single certificate covers the languages, platforms, and uses permitted by its agreement.

4. Permitted uses
Permission is worldwide, nonexclusive, unlimited in number of copies, and indefinite while this agreement is followed. You may copy, typeset, convert formats, narrate, give away, publish, integrate, distribute, and sell the complete translation or accurately identified passages in digital media. This includes files, audiobooks, websites, and free, paid, subscription-based, or ad-supported apps.

You may also print for personal use and use verses, quotations, selections, and individual Bible books in printed materials or products to give away or sell, such as T-shirts, mugs, books, and magazines. You may create your own covers, layouts, illustrations, and other designs. Preserve the wording, references, and attribution under sections 6 and 7. Distributors may set their own prices.

Before printing copies for sale, donation, or other distribution to third parties that contain the complete Bible, the complete Old Testament, or the complete New Testament of a covered translation, register the responsible party free of charge, verify their email address, and obtain written approval from BibliaTodo. This applies to editions in one or more volumes, with or without study material, including print on demand. After verifying the email address, write to info@bibliatodo.org from that address with your reference number, the responsible party’s name, the translation and release used, the purpose of distribution, and a PDF of the final layout including the cover and credits.

BibliaTodo will review the stated purpose, the integrity of the text, attribution, identification of the publisher, and presentation of the work. The review helps ensure that the edition respects the translation and readers can identify who publishes it. Written approval identifies the edition and covers identical reprints. A different cover, layout, or editorial content requires new approval. BibliaTodo reviews and approves editions free of charge and collects no royalties, commissions, or license fees for authorized distribution.

5. No royalties or commissions
BibliaTodo LLC will not charge royalties, commissions, license fees, or subsequent payments for the uses authorized here. Revenue earned by the distributor creates no payment obligation to BibliaTodo. Printing, hosting, store, tax, and contracted-service costs are the distributor's responsibility. BibliaTodo LLC undertakes not to bring claims concerning the exercise of its own rights that is expressly authorized by and carried out in accordance with this agreement.

6. Integrity and identification
The wording of the BibliaTodo translation used must be preserved, including the brackets and other textual elements of the edition received. Typography, arrangement, and formatting may be changed without altering the text. You may add illustrations, coloring drawings, writing spaces, and other elements you own or have permission to use. Passage selections must retain their references and must not be presented as a complete Bible. Commentary, illustrations, and other added material must be clearly distinguished from the BTNT text. Changes to its wording may not be presented as the official BibliaTodo translation and are not authorized by this agreement. Suspected errors may be reported to info@bibliatodo.org.

7. Attribution
Include the credit corresponding to the language used, legibly and accessibly:

«Santa Biblia, Nueva Traducción BibliaTodo (NTBT). Publicada por BibliaTodo Bible Society, sello editorial de BibliaTodo LLC. https://www.bibliatodo.org/es/ntbt/».

«Holy Bible, BibliaTodo New Translation (BTNT). Published by BibliaTodo Bible Society, an imprint of BibliaTodo LLC. https://www.bibliatodo.org/en/btnt/».

«Bíblia Sagrada, Nova Tradução BibliaTodo (NTB-BR). Publicada por BibliaTodo Bible Society, selo editorial da BibliaTodo LLC. https://www.bibliatodo.org/pt/ntb-br/».

In interactive media, link BibliaTodo Bible Society to the corresponding language page. In print or media without links, include the full URL. The credit need not be repeated in every verse or response. Preserve the release identification and acknowledgments included in the materials.

8. Study material and other resources
This authorization includes the Bible text of the original translations published under this agreement and their own introductions and notes when identified as available. Spanish NTBT and English BTNT offer the Bible text and a complete study edition. Portuguese NTB-BR currently offers the Bible text. Plus tools, other translations, images, maps, recordings, and other third-party resources retain their own terms and are not included merely because they appear on BibliaTodo. Distributors may produce their own faithful narration of the text. This grants no access to recordings owned by others.

9. Responsibility and presentation
The distributor is responsible for their product, additions, marketing, and compliance with each platform's rules. Authorization does not grant exclusivity or permit claims that BibliaTodo sponsors, certifies, or endorses the product. The imprint and translation names may be used to identify the material faithfully, without impersonating BibliaTodo or claiming an unverified registered-trademark status. The material is offered in its available state, without promising suitability for a particular printing format or guaranteeing third-party rights.

10. Reference editions and preexisting rights
The conditions of this agreement apply to BTNT's own contributions that BibliaTodo LLC is entitled to authorize. Acknowledgments of OSHB and SBLGNT identify the Hebrew and Greek reference editions and their CC BY 4.0 licenses; they do not attribute ownership of the English BTNT text to those entities. The WLC Masoretic text retains its public-domain status. BTNT registration is not imposed on independent uses of those editions permitted by their licenses or by law. Documentary acknowledgments do not imply endorsement of this translation by their rights holders.

11. Continuity and corrections
Version 1.7 takes effect on its publication date and applies to uses under this version. Earlier authorizations are governed by the terms under which they were granted. Future releases will keep their identifying information. If a breach occurs, BibliaTodo may give notice and allow thirty days to correct it. Permission does not cover uses that remain in breach after that period. This does not limit rights provided by law or by third-party licenses.

12. Personal certificate and data protection
Registration is required for the complete print editions described in section 4 and optional for those who request a personal certificate or editorial notices for directly permitted uses. To issue and recover that document, the applicant provides accurate information, confirms authority to represent an organization where applicable, accepts necessary data processing and verifies their email address. BibliaTodo retains the necessary information, the exact accepted agreement and the relevant dates as evidence of the certificate; no historical agreement is rewritten. Identity documents are not requested and applicants are not subscribed to advertising. Editorial notices require a separate optional choice and may be disabled without losing permission. Requests for information, access or correction may be sent to info@bibliatodo.org. Directly permitted uses remain available without registration, a certificate, or a subscription to notices.
